Free VPN for Android TV

A free Android TV VPN client in Kotlin + Jetpack Compose for TV, built on a real VpnService / TUN foundation with a foreground service, system permission flow, and five observable connection states driven by a single StateFlow. Clean, layered architecture with a swappable WireGuard tunnel seam and privacy-safe logging.
